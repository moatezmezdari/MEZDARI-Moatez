§ 01

Profile

I work at the intersection of design and validation for plastic automotive components — trim, bumpers, emblems, interior parts — carrying them from early feasibility through 3D/2D iteration to production-ready release.

As Interface Leader on the Renault XR110 center console, I coordinate directly between the customer and internal teams, manage engineering changes (ECR/ECO), and keep packaging and interference checks aligned as designs evolve. I'm equally comfortable in a CAD session, a supplier review, or writing the technical documentation that ties both together.

My background spans biomedical prototyping and prosthetics design before automotive — which is where the habit of treating every part as a system of tolerances, materials, and constraints comes from.
